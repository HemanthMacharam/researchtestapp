# AI Agents Face Challenges in Real-World Web Browsing Tasks

**Date:** 2026-09-04  
**Category:** Agents, Data Science

## What Happened

A recent study by Decodo evaluated 45 AI agents across 10 capabilities, focusing on their performance in real-world web browsing tasks. The research found that none of the agents achieved the maximum score of 20 points. Claude for Chrome scored the highest with 18 points, followed by the ChatGPT Chrome Extension with 14 points. The study highlighted significant shortcomings in areas such as form filling, transaction completion, cross-tab awareness, and handling third-party integrations. Notably, agents averaged only 0.43 out of 2 in transaction handling, often initiating purchases but failing to complete them. Additionally, security safeguards for sensitive actions and information, including multi-step workflows, were found to be inadequate or undocumented in many cases. ([techradar.com](https://www.techradar.com/pro/research-finds-ai-agents-havent-quite-mastered-real-world-browsing-tasks-despite-claiming-they-can?utm_source=openai))

## Why It Matters

The findings underscore a critical gap between the capabilities AI vendors claim and the actual performance of their agents in real-world scenarios. As AI agents are increasingly integrated into various applications, their inability to perform fundamental web browsing tasks reliably poses significant challenges. This discrepancy can lead to inefficiencies, security vulnerabilities, and a lack of trust in AI-driven solutions. Addressing these shortcomings is essential for the broader adoption and effective deployment of AI agents in complex, real-world environments.

## Technical Details

The Decodo study assessed AI agents based on their ability to autonomously perform tasks such as filling out forms, completing transactions, maintaining cross-tab awareness, and managing third-party integrations. The agents were evaluated across 10 different capabilities, with none achieving the maximum score of 20 points. Claude for Chrome led with 18 points, while the ChatGPT Chrome Extension scored 14 points. A significant issue identified was in transaction handling, where agents averaged only 0.43 out of 2, often initiating purchases but failing to complete them. Furthermore, many agents lacked adequate security safeguards for sensitive actions and information, including multi-step workflows. ## Practical Example

Consider an AI agent integrated into an e-commerce platform designed to assist users in completing purchases. In the study, agents often initiated the purchase process but failed to complete transactions, highlighting a critical flaw in their design. For instance, an agent might successfully add items to a shopping cart and proceed to the checkout page but fail to process payment information or confirm the order, leaving the transaction incomplete. This failure not only disrupts the user experience but also undermines the reliability of AI agents in handling complex, multi-step processes.

## Use Cases

- **E-commerce Platforms:** AI agents can assist customers in browsing products, adding items to carts, and completing purchases. However, as demonstrated in the study, their current limitations in transaction handling can hinder these processes.

- **Customer Support:** AI agents are deployed to handle customer inquiries, process returns, and manage service requests. Their inability to manage multi-step workflows effectively can lead to incomplete resolutions and customer dissatisfaction.

- **Healthcare Systems:** AI agents are used to schedule appointments, process patient information, and manage records. Inadequate handling of sensitive information and multi-step processes can compromise patient care and data security.

## Limitations / Risks

- **Security Vulnerabilities:** The study found that many AI agents lack adequate security safeguards for sensitive actions and information, including multi-step workflows. This can lead to data breaches and unauthorized access.

- **Operational Inefficiencies:** Agents failing to complete transactions or manage complex tasks can disrupt business operations, leading to lost revenue and decreased productivity.

- **Erosion of Trust:** Persistent failures in AI agents can erode user trust, making them hesitant to adopt AI-driven solutions in critical areas.
