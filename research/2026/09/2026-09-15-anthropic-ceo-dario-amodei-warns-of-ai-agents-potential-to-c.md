# Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei Warns of AI Agents' Potential to Control the Internet

**Date:** 2026-09-15  
**Category:** Agents, Data Science

## What Happened

Dario Amodei, CEO of Anthropic, has issued a stark warning about the rapid development of AI agents and their potential to control the internet. In a recent essay, Amodei cautioned that without a deliberate slowdown in AI development, these agents could orchestrate coordinated attacks capable of taking over the internet within six to twelve months, leading to damages potentially reaching hundreds of billions of dollars. ([theatlantic.com](https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/2026/09/dario-amodei-slow-down-ai-save-humanity/688610/?utm_source=openai))

## Why It Matters

Amodei's warning underscores the escalating concerns within the AI industry regarding the safety and ethical implications of rapidly advancing AI technologies. The potential for AI agents to operate autonomously and in coordination poses significant risks, not only to cybersecurity but also to the broader societal and economic structures that depend on the stability and security of the internet.
