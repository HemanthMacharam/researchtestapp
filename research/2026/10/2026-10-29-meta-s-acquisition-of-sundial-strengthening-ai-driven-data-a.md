# Meta's Acquisition of Sundial: Strengthening AI-Driven Data Analytics

**Date:** 2026-10-29  
**Category:** Agents, Data Science

## What Happened

On October 23, 2026, Meta announced the acquisition of Sundial, an AI-powered data analytics platform founded by two former Meta employees. This strategic move aims to bolster Meta's data analytics capabilities, particularly in the realm of artificial intelligence. Sundial's platform is designed to help businesses quickly access reliable data analysis, a feature that will be integrated into Meta's newly established data analytics organization. This unit is led by Alex Schultz, Meta's former chief marketing officer, who was appointed as the company's first-ever chief data officer in July 2026. Schultz oversees the Data Analytics Tooling & Agents unit, known as DATA, which focuses on advancing Meta's analytics capabilities. In a LinkedIn post announcing the integration, Schultz emphasized that AI has created effectively unlimited demand for insights and stated Meta's ambition to define the future of AI analytics. ## Why It Matters

The acquisition of Sundial signifies Meta's commitment to enhancing its data analytics infrastructure, particularly in the AI domain. By integrating Sundial's platform, Meta aims to provide faster and more reliable data-driven insights, catering to the growing demand for AI analytics. This move also reflects a broader industry trend where companies are investing heavily in AI to maintain a competitive edge. For instance, Amazon reported a significant decline in free cash flow due to substantial investments in AI infrastructure, highlighting the financial implications of such strategic decisions. ## Technical Details

Sundial's platform utilizes advanced AI algorithms to process and analyze large datasets, enabling businesses to derive actionable insights efficiently. The integration into Meta's DATA unit suggests a focus on developing tools and systems that leverage AI to deliver data-driven insights. While specific technical details of the integration have not been disclosed, the emphasis on AI-driven analytics indicates a move towards more sophisticated data processing capabilities within Meta's ecosystem.
