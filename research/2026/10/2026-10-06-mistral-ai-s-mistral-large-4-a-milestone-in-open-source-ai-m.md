# Mistral AI's Mistral Large 4: A Milestone in Open-Source AI Models

**Date:** 2026-10-06
**Category:** Agents, Data Science

## What Happened

On October 6, 2026, Mistral AI, a French startup, unveiled Mistral Large 4, a 1-trillion-parameter multimodal AI model. This model is designed to perform a wide range of tasks, including finance, cybersecurity, and geospatial analysis, positioning Mistral AI as a significant competitor to leading Chinese AI models. Mistral Large 4 is set to be released as open-source on October 27, 2026, with a monitored interface already available. ## Technical Details

Mistral Large 4 is a 1-trillion-parameter multimodal model, with 49 billion parameters active at any given time. It was trained over two months using 4,000 Nvidia Grace Blackwell GPUs in Europe, showcasing a significant technical achievement in AI model development. The model is designed to handle a variety of tasks, including finance, cybersecurity, and geospatial analysis, and is expected to be released as open-source on October 27, 2026.
