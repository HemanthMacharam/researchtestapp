# OpenAI's Acquisition of Orchestra Bio: A Strategic Move into AI-Driven Drug Development

**Date:** 2026-10-20
**Category:** Agents, Data Science

## What Happened

OpenAI has acquired Orchestra Bio, a biotech startup specializing in developing AI tools to assist pharmaceutical companies in managing drug development processes. This acquisition aims to integrate Orchestra Bio's team and technology into OpenAI's Rosalind platform, which provides AI-driven solutions tailored for life sciences researchers. The move reflects a broader trend among AI leaders, including competitors like Anthropic, to expand into scientific domains such as biology and chemistry.
